Our Mission

At Fernwood Plant Co., our mission is to make plant parenthood simple and joyful. We source healthy, responsibly grown plants, pair them with thoughtfully made planters, and share honest care advice so every plant we sell has a long, happy life.

Our Story

Fernwood began in 2019 on a cramped apartment windowsill, where our founder nursed a single cutting of pothos back to life. Friends started asking for cuttings, then for advice, and soon the windowsill had become a weekend market stall.

Today we're a small team working from a sunny greenhouse storefront, but our approach hasn't changed: fewer, better plants, and a real person ready to help when a leaf starts to yellow.
